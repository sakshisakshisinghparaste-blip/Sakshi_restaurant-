Founders & Philosophies

Crafting raw thermal drama into effortless contemporary comfort.

Founded by Executive Chef Laurent Vance—formerly culinary director across Parisian three-star dining rooms—and Master Sommelier Elena Vance, AURA was born from an unyielding devotion to primal gastronomy.

Every dish centers on our custom-engineered 9-foot open brick hearth, burning seasoned California white oak, fruitwood, and binchotan charcoal. We collaborate with generational regenerative farmers to secure rare heritage legumes, dry-aged heritage beef, and line-caught seafood hours after harvest.
